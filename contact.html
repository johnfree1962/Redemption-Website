Frequently Asked Questions

Can I transfer my prescription here?

Yes! We accept prescriptions from any pharmacy. Simply bring your prescription or contact us, and we'll handle the transfer for you.

Do you offer delivery services?

Yes, we offer same-day and next-day delivery services. Free delivery is available on orders over $50 within the city.

What insurance do you accept?

We accept most major health insurance plans. Please contact us with your insurance information for verification.

Can I get free consultations?

Absolutely! Our licensed pharmacists provide free consultations on medications and health-related questions.

Do you offer generic medications?

Yes! We offer generic alternatives for most medications at significantly lower prices.

How do I refill my prescriptions?

You can refill in person, by phone, or through our website. We also offer auto-refill services for convenience.
